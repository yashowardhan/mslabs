How the partnership works

Simple enough to fit into the way your agency already works.

1. You send the brief

  • Project scope, designs, requirements or an existing codebase
  • Preferred stack and workflow
  • Any client deadlines or constraints

2. We define the delivery

  • Fixed scope, milestone or capacity-based engagement
  • Clear assumptions and acceptance criteria
  • NDA/confidentiality where required

3. Your workflow stays intact

  • GitHub / GitLab, Jira / Linear, Slack and your existing process
  • PR-based development and testing
  • Direct technical communication

4. You own the client relationship

  • White-label delivery available
  • No direct client selling or account management
  • You decide how MS Labs appears to your client
