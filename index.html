Questions

What happens if my site breaks?

I fix it. Plan members get priority response and recovery from the latest backup. Updates are backed up first and checked afterwards, and Plus and Pro add a staging site so updates can be tested beforehand.

What does the free site check include?

Three specific findings about your site's speed, security or updates, sent by email with a short suggested fix for each. No obligation. I only look at what is publicly visible.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Care plans are month to month and you can cancel any time.

Can you work with my current hosting?

Yes, I work with most hosts. I will need access to your WordPress admin and, for some tasks, your hosting account.

What isn't covered by a care plan?

New site builds and larger feature projects are quoted separately. The developer hours in each plan cover small fixes and edits, and extra hours are available if you need them.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I can work under your brand, take on overflow development, and look after your clients' sites. Prepaid hour packs are available.

Do you support WooCommerce stores?

Yes. Care Pro includes WooCommerce and custom plugin monitoring, which suits stores where downtime costs sales.
