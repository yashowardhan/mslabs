The problem

A slow, bloated site costs you enquiries and sales.

Most site owners do not find out until the leads quietly stop. These are the problems I see most often.

  • Slow mobile load times

    Heavy images, render-blocking scripts and layout shifts make visitors leave before they read a word.

  • Bloated plugins and themes

    Years of plugins stacked on a page builder slow every request and widen your security exposure.

  • Missed sales and enquiries

    Contact forms that land in spam, checkouts that stall and pages that never rank all cost you revenue.

  • Missing security basics

    Outdated updates, absent security headers and no tested backups turn small issues into emergencies.

  • No senior engineer on call

    Hiring in-house is expensive, and juggling freelancers means nobody owns the whole picture.

  • Patchwork inherited code

    Custom code nobody documented is hard to change and easy to break. It needs someone who can read it.

Before you start

Questions

What does the free website audit include?

The free audit looks at what is publicly visible on your site: mobile load times, WordPress bottlenecks, security headers and a short list of top priority fixes. It is a quick diagnostic, not a full audit. Run it here.

Why pay for the Site Health Check if the free audit is available?

The free audit gives you headline findings. The $149 Site Health Check is a fuller written review with a prioritized fix list and practical recommendations.

How do you handle website access?

I prefer named user accounts with the minimum access needed and two-factor authentication where available. I do not need your main password when your platform supports separate accounts. Access should be removed when it is no longer required.

Can you work with my current hosting and custom code?

Yes. I can usually work with your existing host, theme, plugins and custom code. Access requirements depend on the task.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

Care plans are month to month. Larger development projects are quoted separately with their own scope and terms.

What happens if you are unavailable?

MS Labs is a solo senior-engineer studio, so capacity is intentionally limited. Planned unavailability is communicated in advance. I do not promise 24/7 on-call coverage.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Agency partnerships can be white-label, fixed-scope or ongoing capacity. The agency keeps the client relationship. See the dedicated Agency Partner page.
