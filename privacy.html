Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

MS Labs Studio is operated by Yash Kaushik from Gurgaon, India. This policy explains what information is collected through this website and why.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, I may receive your name, email address, website address, company or agency name, the service you select and the message you provide. This information is used to respond to you and, where relevant, prepare or deliver the requested service.

Form processing

Website enquiry forms are submitted through a third-party form delivery service configured to forward submissions to contact@mslabsstudio.com. The provider may process the submitted information to deliver the form. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive credentials through the enquiry form.

Website access

If you become a client, technical access should normally be provided through named accounts with the minimum permissions needed and two-factor authentication where available. Access should be removed when it is no longer required.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the site does not intentionally use advertising cookies or a marketing tracking platform. Third-party services used to load or process website functionality may have their own technical logs and privacy policies.

Retention

I retain enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide services, maintain business records and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data. Contact me at contact@mslabsstudio.com to make a request.

International processing

MS Labs Studio is based in India and may work with international clients. Information submitted through third-party services may be processed in countries where those providers operate.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to contact@mslabsstudio.com.
